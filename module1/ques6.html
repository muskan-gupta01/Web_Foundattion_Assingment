About Our University

Invertis University is located in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India. It offers educational opportunities in various academic fields.

The university provides an academic environment where students can develop their knowledge, technical skills, and professional abilities.

  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech)
  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA)
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA)
  • Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
